# CFD

To obtain an accurate aerodynamic model for use in the full MATLAB/Simulink control simulation, aerodynamic coefficients were calculated in ANSYS Fluent. RAS Aero II was also initially used as a much less computationally expensive tool, but lacks the alpha sweep (0-15º only) and unique characteristics of Aeolus (EDF tube, complex intake geometry and airflow interactions). Missile DATCOM was also considered but it is unobtainable due to ITAR restrictions.
